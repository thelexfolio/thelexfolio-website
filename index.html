Questions

Asked often.

अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Is my money held by LexFolio?

No. Your investment goes directly to the mutual fund house (AMC), in your own name and folio. LexFolio facilitates the process and services your folio.

How do you earn? Is it a regular or a direct plan?

When you invest through a distributor, you invest in the regular plan of a scheme. The fund house pays us a commission from the scheme's expenses, so a regular plan has a slightly higher expense ratio than its direct plan. We will show you this before you invest.

Will you tell me which fund to buy?

We explain how schemes and categories work, what they cost and the risks involved. The choice of scheme is always yours, and we carry it out.

What do I need for KYC?

Usually your PAN, Aadhaar or another address proof, a photograph and your bank details. We confirm the exact list with you before you start.

Can we talk in Hindi? हिंदी में बात कर सकते हैं?

जी हाँ। हम हिंदी और अंग्रेज़ी, दोनों में बात करते हैं। Every call and document walkthrough is available in either language.
